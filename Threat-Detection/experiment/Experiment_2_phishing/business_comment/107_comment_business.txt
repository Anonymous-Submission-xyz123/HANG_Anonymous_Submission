**Core Use Cases**

**1. Spam Detection Alert to IT Security Operations**  
The email content matches patterns of unsolicited commercial messaging and is automatically flagged by the organization’s email security gateway. An alert is generated and routed to the IT security operations center, enabling rapid verification, quarantine, and remediation actions to protect the corporate mail environment from potential abuse or malicious payloads.

**2. End‑User Awareness Notification**  
A templated notification is dispatched to all employees informing them of the identified spam attempt, describing its characteristics (e.g., deceptive phone numbers, explicit language) and reminding users of safe email practices. This reinforces corporate policy on handling unsolicited communications and reduces the likelihood of user interaction with malicious content.

**3. Email Filtering Policy Enforcement Log**  
The incident is recorded in the centralized email filtering policy audit log, documenting the source, detection rules triggered, and actions taken (quarantine, delete, or tag). This log supports compliance reporting, trend analysis, and continuous improvement of filtering rules across the organization’s messaging infrastructure.

**4. Incident Reporting and Documentation for Compliance**  
The detection event is automatically filed into the organization’s incident management system, assigning a reference number and notifying compliance officers. This ensures that any regulatory requirements for tracking unsolicited communications are met and provides an audit trail for future investigations or external audits.

**Operational Benefits**
- **Enhanced Email Hygiene:** Immediate identification and containment of unsolicited messages preserve inbox integrity and reduce exposure to phishing or malware.  
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Systematic logging and reporting satisfy legal and industry obligations for monitoring unsolicited communications.  
- **User Empowerment:** Proactive awareness messages educate staff, decreasing the risk of accidental engagement with malicious content.  
- **Operational Efficiency:** Automated alerts and documentation streamline the response workflow, minimizing manual effort for security and compliance teams.
